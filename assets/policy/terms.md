# Terms

## The short version

Pesa Clarity is a free app that turns your M-Pesa and bank messages
into a simple daily budget. It helps you plan; the decisions stay yours.
By using the app you agree to these terms and to our privacy policy.

## Who can use it

Pesa Clarity is for people aged 18 and over, using their own phone and
their own accounts.

## What the app does

- It reads messages and statements from the accounts you choose, on your phone.
- It suggests categories, budgets and a safe-to-spend figure from what it reads.
- Messages it is still learning wait for you to check.

Pesa Clarity is a budgeting tool. It is not a bank or a lender, and it
never moves money. Its figures are estimates to help you plan, not
financial advice. Check anything important against your statements.

## Your part

- Keep your phone, PIN and vault passphrase safe. Only you know the passphrase, so keep a copy somewhere secure.
- Correct anything the app reads wrongly, so your budget stays right.
- Use the app and our server only as they are meant to be used.

## Your account

An account is only used for sign-in and backups, and you can use the
app without one. You can delete your account from inside the app at any
time. We may pause an account that is used to attack or overload our
server.
